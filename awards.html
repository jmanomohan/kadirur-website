Awards & Recognition

Kadirur’s major panchayat and bank awards, all in one place.

Kadirur Grama Panchayat and the Kadirur Service Co-Operative Bank have both earned major awards at state and national levels. This page brings those recognitions together in a clean, readable format.

Recent Panchayat Awards

Kadirur Grama Panchayat honors

Atma Nirbhar Panchayat Award

Kadirur Grama Panchayat secured 3rd place nationally in the Atma Nirbhar Panchayat Special Award category of the National Panchayat Awards.

National recognition

The panchayat was recognized for work in poverty alleviation, healthcare, waste management, and women’s empowerment.

ISO certification

Kadirur Panchayat also achieved ISO certification for good governance and localization of sustainable development goals.

Recent Bank Awards

Kadirur Service Co-Operative Bank honors

The Kadirur Service Co-Operative Bank has earned multiple recent honors for growth, performance, and cooperative excellence.

Its recognitions reflect strong deposit growth, loan disbursement, agricultural support, and broader cooperative banking performance.
