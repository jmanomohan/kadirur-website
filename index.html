KANNUR DISTRICT, KERALA

A warm suburb with strong literacy, growing mobility, and deep local character.

Kadirur is a census town and suburb of Thalassery in Kerala, located around 8 km from Thalassery town on the Tellicherry-Mysore road corridor.

Connectivity

How people reach Kadirur

By Road

State Highway 30 (Thalassery-Coorg road) passes through Kadirur, with smooth access to Thalassery and onward links to NH 66.

By Rail

The nearest major railway access is Thalassery on the Mangaluru-Palakkad line, connected to major Indian cities.

By Air

Kannur International Airport is the closest airport. Calicut International Airport is also an option for domestic and international routes.

Quick answers about Kadirur

Where is Kadirur located?

Kadirur is in Kannur district, Kerala, and functions as a suburb of Thalassery.

How far is it from Thalassery?

It is approximately 8 km from Thalassery town.

Is literacy high in Kadirur?

Yes. Census data reports literacy around 97.7%, above the Kerala average.

Which road is most important here?

SH 30, popularly known as the Thalassery-Coorg route, is a key corridor.
